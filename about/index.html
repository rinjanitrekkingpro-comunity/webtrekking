Our Story

Rinjani Trekking Pro:
A Legacy of the Mountain & Community

Our Roots: More Than Just Guides

We are direct descendants of the first guides in Senaru and Sembalun villages. For us, Mount Rinjani is not just a tourist destination—it is our ancestral home, cultural heritage, and a sacred place we have protected for generations.

We are not middlemen or third-party agencies. We are native Lombok locals who know every curve of the trail, the direction of the wind, and the natural secrets of Rinjani better than anyone else.

Why Book Directly With Us?

By booking directly with local experts, you cut out the middlemen. This ensures that your travel expenses directly impact:

  • Village Economy: Revitalizing local small businesses around the slopes of Rinjani.
  • Team Welfare: Providing fair wages and work protection for our guides and porters.
  • Social Projects: Funding village children's education and the development of local public facilities.
