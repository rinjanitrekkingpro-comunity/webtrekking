Best Time to Hike Mount Rinjani: Seasonal Guide 2026

April 5, 2026 | Rinjani Trekking Pro

One of the most critical factors determining the success and comfort of your Mount Rinjani trek is not your fitness level or gear — it is when you choose to go. Rinjani is deeply influenced by seasonal weather; the same trail can feel remarkably different depending on rainfall, wind patterns, and conditions inside the caldera.

As local guides who accompany hundreds of hikers up and down Rinjani every year, we are frequently asked: "Which month is truly the best to hike?" This article answers that question thoroughly, including the official opening and closing schedule of Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR) — essential information to know before you plan your journey.

Understanding the Seasons Around Rinjani

Lombok Island has two main seasons that directly affect trail conditions: the dry season and the wet season. Each creates a very different hiking experience on Rinjani.

Dry Season (Approximately April–November)

This is the ideal period to hike Mount Rinjani. Trails remain dry, the risk of landslides and slippery paths drops significantly, and your chances of enjoying unobstructed sunrises and sunsets — free from heavy fog — are excellent. This is the official trekking season, when Mount Rinjani National Park fully opens all public trails.

Wet Season (Approximately December–March)

During the wet season, Mount Rinjani National Park generally closes hiking trails for public safety. Heavy rainfall dramatically increases the risk of landslides, slippery rock and mud, thick fog that severely reduces visibility, and flash floods along sections of the trail leading toward Segara Anak Lake. Throughout this period, nearly all trekking operators — including us — suspend departures, prioritizing your safety above all else.

Best Months to Hike Mount Rinjani

Based on years of experience guiding hikers, here is our detailed recommendation:

  • June–August: Peak dry season. The weather is most stable, skies are clearest, and sunrise views are at their absolute best. Note: this is also the busiest period — we recommend booking well in advance to secure your spot.
  • April–May & September–November: Shoulder season. Weather remains excellent, trails are far less crowded, and trekking package prices are generally more affordable.
  • December–March: Generally avoided due to unpredictable and severe weather. Only consider these months if the National Park authority officially announces an exceptional opening — which is extremely rare.

Mount Rinjani National Park Opening & Closing Schedule

Please note: exact opening and closing dates may vary slightly each year, based on official assessments of trail safety and weather conditions.

Before finalizing your travel dates, always confirm the latest trail status directly with a local trekking operator. Official announcements reach on-site guides based in Senaru and Sembalun much faster than outdated information found on general websites.

Practical Tips for Choosing Your Trekking Dates

  • Avoid long public holidays if you prefer a quieter, more peaceful journey — trails and campsites become extremely crowded during national holiday periods.
  • Check the moon phase. Hiking during a full moon provides natural brightness during early morning summit attempts — though a headlamp remains mandatory at all times.
  • Book at least 2–4 weeks in advance during peak season (June–August), since daily hiker quotas are strictly limited by the National Park to protect the environment.
  • Ask your local guide about current trail conditions, especially at the very beginning or end of the dry season, when transitional weather can shift unexpectedly.

Why Confirming with Local Guides Matters Most

General weather forecasts found online are often inaccurate for mountain microclimates such as Rinjani. Caldeira winds, rim fog, and loose volcanic sand conditions on the summit route can change dramatically within just a few hours. Local guides who live and work year-round at the foot of Rinjani understand seasonal weather patterns far better than generic forecasts — and can help you select the safest, most comfortable dates for your trek.

Conclusion

The absolute best time to hike Mount Rinjani is during the dry season. For the most stable weather, aim between June and August. For quieter trails and better value, choose April–May or September–November.
