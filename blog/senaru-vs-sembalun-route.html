Senaru vs Sembalun: Which Route Is Best for Your Rinjani Trek?

April 3, 2026 | Rinjani Trekking Pro

The most common question asked by every new hiker is: "Should I go via Senaru or Sembalun?" It is a fair question — these two routes offer remarkably different terrain, views, difficulty levels, and cultural experiences.

As local guides who have accompanied thousands of hikers from around the world, we break down the key differences to help you choose the trail that best matches your fitness, schedule, and goals.

The Senaru Route

Starting from Senaru Village on Rinjani’s northern slope — also known as the gateway to Sendang Gile Waterfall — this trail winds through dense, cool tropical rainforest. The canopy provides welcome shade during the early hours of the hike.

Advantages of Senaru Route:

  • Cool, shaded terrain under forest cover.
  • Direct access to Segara Anak Lake and natural hot springs.
  • Ideal for hikers who prefer caldera views without necessarily summiting.
  • Immersive Sasak village culture and hospitality.

Challenges:

  • Descent toward the lake can be steep and slippery, especially in wet season.
  • Longer hiking distance on Day 1 to reach the rim campsite.

The Sembalun Route

Located on Rinjani’s eastern side, starting from Sembalun Village surrounded by vast high-altitude savannah. Unlike Senaru, this trail is wide open, offering panoramic views across the grasslands and mountain ridges throughout the journey.

Advantages of Sembalun Route:

  • The standard and most popular route for reaching the summit.
  • Spectacular open savannah scenery, especially at sunrise and sunset.
  • Gentler gradient during the first half of the trek compared to Senaru.

Challenges:

  • Minimal shade — hot and exposed during daytime.
  • The final summit push consists of loose volcanic sand and gravel; physically demanding.

Recommended Combination Route

If you have 3–4 days, we strongly recommend the traverse route: up via Sembalun for the summit at sunrise, then down through Senaru to visit the lake and hot springs. This is the ultimate Rinjani experience — conquering the peak, exploring the caldera, and relaxing in natural hot water — all in one journey.

Conclusion
