When is the Best Time to Climb Mount Rinjani?
Months & Seasons Guide 2026

Published by Rinjani Trekking Pro Team | 5 Min Read

Many prospective trekkers ask us: "When is the absolute best time to hike Mount Rinjani?" Timing your trek correctly is crucial. It directly impacts your personal safety, hiking comfort, and of course, the stunning clarity of the views you will experience from the crater rim and summit.

The Mount Rinjani National Park authority opens the official climbing trails only during specific months of the year, depending on seasonal weather patterns and ecosystem safety. Here is your complete guide to planning your 2026 hike:

Best Season: April to October (Dry Season)

This is the most highly recommended window for scaling Mount Rinjani. During these dry months, weather conditions are optimal for high-altitude trekking.

  • Clear Blue Skies: Clear conditions mean unobstructed panoramas of Segara Anak Lake, Mount Baru Jari volcano, and spectacular golden sunrises spanning Bali and Sumbawa.
  • Safe & Dry Trails: Rain is rare, meaning paths are firm, less slippery, and landslide risk is extremely low.
  • Official Opening Period: Mount Rinjani National Park officially grants trekking permits throughout this window.
  • Peak Months (July to September): These are the most popular months. Humidity is low, daytime walking temperatures are pleasant, and rainfall chance is near zero.

Wet Season: November to March (Park Closure)

During this period, official trekking trails inside the National Park are typically closed for safety and forest restoration.

  • Heavy Tropical Rainfall: Frequent downpours make loose volcanic scree and steep slopes slippery and dangerous.
  • Thick Fog & Poor Visibility: Dense clouds obscure views and hamper trail navigation.
  • Increased Environmental Risks: High risk of flash floods in river valleys and landslides along steep ridge lines.

Important Schedule Note for 2026

Always check official announcements from the Mount Rinjani National Park Authority (TNGRN). Opening and closing dates can shift slightly based on real-time weather assessments and conservation policies.
