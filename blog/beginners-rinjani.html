Beginner’s Guide to Hiking Mount Rinjani
Without Extreme Fatigue

Published by Rinjani Trekking Pro Team | 6 Min Read

Is this your first time attempting to conquer Indonesia's second-highest volcano? There is no need to feel overwhelmed! With proper physical preparation and smart hiking strategies, even novice trekkers can stand proudly at the 3,726m summit or enjoy the tranquil beauty of Segara Anak Crater Lake.

Here are 4 proven, expert tips to prevent premature exhaustion and keep your energy high throughout the journey:

1 Pace Yourself: "Pole-Pole" (Slow & Steady)

The biggest mistake beginners make is starting too fast. Trekking is an endurance test, not a sprint. Maintain a rhythmic, steady pace. It is far better to walk slowly without stopping than to march quickly and rest every 5 minutes.

2 Maintain Continuous Hydration & Nutrition

Sip water frequently in small amounts rather than gulping down large quantities when thirsty. Carry high-energy snacks like chocolates, energy bars, or nuts to keep your blood sugar and stamina stable along steep slopes.

3 Pack Light (Bring Only What You Need)

Every extra kilogram in your backpack feels 3 times heavier at high altitudes. Carry only your daypack with essentials like water, rain jacket, camera, and personal medicine.

4 Rely on Professional Guide & Porter Support

This is the single most effective way to enjoy your trek without burning out. Let experienced local porters carry heavy camping gear, food supplies, and tents while you focus on enjoying the views and preserving your energy.
