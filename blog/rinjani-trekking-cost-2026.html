Mount Rinjani Trekking Cost Estimate 2026:
What Exactly Is Included?

Published by Rinjani Trekking Pro Team | 5 Min Read

One of the most frequent questions prospective hikers ask is: "how Much does it actually cost to trek Mount Rinjani?" Prices can vary significantly based on your trip duration (e.g., 2D1N, 3D2N, or 4D3N), your chosen route, group size, and the level of camping comfort provided.

To ensure you budget accurately and avoid falling victim to misleading cheap offers with hidden costs, here is the complete, official price breakdown for 2026.

What is Standardly Included in a Legitimate Package?

A fully licensed and comprehensive trekking package should always cover the following essentials:

  • Official Permits & Tickets: National Park entrance fees (IDR 250,000/day) and mandatory trekker medical checks.
  • Certified Professional Team: Licensed English-speaking mountain guides and experienced porters to carry group gear and heavy supplies.
  • Full Camping Equipment: High-grade double-layer tents, sleeping bags, mattresses, pillows, and shared/private toilet tents.
  • Fresh Meals & Drinks: 3 hot cooked meals daily, fresh tropical fruits, snacks, tea/coffee, and unlimited mineral water.
  • Ground Transport: Private or shared AC vehicle transfers from Lombok airport, Senggigi, Kuta, or harbors to Senaru basecamp.

Red Flags: Beware of Unusually Cheap Offers

Be cautious of "budget" operators offering unrealistically low prices. Unusually cheap packages often omit official park entrance tickets, provide inadequate food rations, or hire unregistered porters. This poses serious safety risks and frequently results in unexpected out-of-pocket expenses midway through your climb.
