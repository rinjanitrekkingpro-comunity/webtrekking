Official Mount Rinjani Regulations 2026:
Permits, Insurance & Strict Rules

Published by Rinjani Trekking Pro Team | 5 Min Read

Every year, the Mount Rinjani National Park Authority (TNGRN) updates its official trekking guidelines to safeguard trekker safety and preserve the delicate volcanic ecosystem. Before booking your flight to Lombok, it is vital to understand the latest 2026 rules to ensure a smooth, legal, and hassle-free expedition.

1. Mandatory Official Trekking Permits (e-Rinjani)

Every hiker must hold an official quota-based permit registered via the National Park system. Unregistered or illegal hiking is strictly prohibited and subject to severe fines and immediate expulsion. Permits must strictly match your designated trail and entry date.

2. Trekking Medical Check & Insurance

All trekkers must undergo a health checkup at the trail Basecam before ascending. Additionally, medical cover and emergency evacuation insurance are mandatory requirements included in all official registration processes.

3. Strict National Park Prohibitions

  • Littering Penalties: Strict Zero-Waste policy. All plastic waste brought up must be packed out and audited at the finish line.
  • Wildlife & Flora Protection: Disturbing wild monkeys, feeding animals, or picking protected Edelweiss flowers is illegal.
  • Fire Hazards: Campfires are strictly forbidden outside designated pit areas to prevent forest fires.
  • OOff-Trail Hiking: Hikers must strictly remain on designated marked paths. Solo hiking without a certified guide is forbidden.
