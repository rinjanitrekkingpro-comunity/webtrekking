Gear & Preparation Checklist

Essential Mount Rinjani Packing List
for a Safe & Comfortable Hike 2026

Published by Rinjani Trekking Pro Team | 6 Min Read

Climbing Mount Rinjani is far more than a casual walk in the park. Rising to an impressive elevation of 3,726 meters (12,224 feet) above sea level, this volcano demands respect. You will encounter diverse terrains and rapidly shifting mountain weather — from hot, dusty savannas during the day to freezing wind chills near freezing point at night.

Packing the right gear is the single most crucial factor in ensuring your trek is safe, comfortable, and truly enjoyable. Bringing inadequate equipment can make your hike unnecessarily exhausting or even dangerous. Here is our official, comprehensive checklist of everything you need before stepping onto the trail:

1. Personal Clothing & Hiking Gear

  • 40–60 Liter Backpack (Daypack / Carrier): Spacious enough for your personal items, water, and camera gear with good back support.
  • Windproof & Waterproof Jacket: Essential for blocking strong summit ridge winds and sudden alpine rains.
  • Warm Thermal Layer / Fleece: Night temperatures at the Crater Rim and Summit can drop to 0°C - 5°C.
  • Trekking Pants: Lightweight, tear-resistant, quick-dry trousers for comfortable movement.
  • Quick-Dry Shirts (3-4 sets): Moisture-wicking breathable base layers to keep sweat away from your body.
  • Warm Gloves & Beanie / Sun Hat: Protects your head and hands against extreme freezing winds during the 02:00 AM summit push.
  • Sturdy Trekking Boots: Waterproof hiking shoes with solid ankle support and good rubber grip for volcanic scree.
  • Thick Woolen Socks (3+ pairs): Keeps feet warm at night and prevents painful blisters during long descents.

2. Campsite & Sleeping Equipment

  • Double-Layer Dome Tent: Keeps you dry during rain and blocks freezing high-altitude winds.
  • Comfortable Mattress & Pillow: Insulates your body from the cold ground for a good night's rest.
  • Warm Down Sleeping Bag: Rated for zero-degree mountain weather.

3. Essential Accessories & Safety Items

  • Headlamp + Spare Batteries: Absolutely crucial for night hiking and navigating camp after dark.
  • Reusable Water Bottle (Min. 2 Liters): To stay constantly hydrated along dry savanna trails.
  • Personal Medical Kit: Painkillers, anti-nausea, band-aids, muscle balm, and personal prescription medicine.
  • Trekking Poles: Reduces strain on your knees by up to 30% during steep ascents and loose scree descents.
  • Trash Bag: Follow the National Park rule — strictly pack out all garbage you produce!
