Package Overview

Hiking and camping at Crater Rim Stampol (2,450 meters above sea level) is the perfect adventure for beginner hikers, families, or travelers looking for a scenic hike when Mount Rinjani National Park main routes are closed during the rainy season.

Day by Day Journey

Detailed Trekking Itinerary

Soft trekking schedule via shady rainforests to Crater Rim Stampol (2,450m).

01
Day One

Senaru Village to Stampol Crater Rim

⏱️ Est. 5 - 6 Hours Hiking
  • Pickup from hotel & transfer to Senaru basecamp.
  • Register at local village office of Teres Genit.
  • Begin hiking through shady, cool tropical rainforest.
  • Enjoy fresh cooked hot lunch served by porters along the forest trail.
  • Arrive at Crater Rim Stampol (2,450m) by late afternoon, set up camp, and enjoy sunset over the valley.
Standard Sharing Service: Group pickup, standard tent & sleeping gear, group cooked lunch.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private AC vehicle, custom pacing, trail outdoor dining table/chairs, 8cm mattress, pillow & private toilet tent.
02
Day Two

Crisp Morning Sunrise & Forest Descent

⏱️ Est. 4 Hours Hiking
  • Wake up early to crisp mountain air & sunrise overlooking the valley.
  • Enjoy a warm hot breakfast prepared by our porter team.
  • Pack up camp (Zero-Waste commitment) and begin a relaxed descent through rainforest.
  • Arrive at Senaru Village around lunchtime and transfer via car to your next destination.
Standard Sharing Service: Shared car transfer to Senggigi, Mataram, Kuta, or Harbor.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private VIP AC vehicle drop-off, FREE Speedboat ticket to Gili Islands, FREE Senaru Waterfall tour.
