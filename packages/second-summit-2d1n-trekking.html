Package Overview

Looking for an unforgettable high-altitude adventure in Lombok? The Senaru Second Summit trekking package (2 Days 1 Night) is the perfect choice. This itinerary takes you to the magnificent Senaru Crater Rim and the second-highest summit of Mount Rinjani (Sangkarang / Senaru Summit at 3,126m).

Experience a majestic sunset over Mount Agung in Bali, witness the breathtaking Segara Anak lake, and trek through Lombok's lush tropical rainforest. It is a brilliant, less crowded alternative to the main peak, offering a highly rewarding high-altitude climb.

Day by Day Journey

Detailed Trekking Itinerary

2-day high-altitude trek via Senaru rainforest up to Sangkarang Peak (3,126m).

01
Day One

Senaru Village (601m) to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m)

⏱️ Est. 6 - 7 Hours Hiking
  • 06:30 AM: Enjoy breakfast at your hotel to fuel up for the trek.
  • 07:45 AM: Walk to Rinjani Trek Center (RTC) in Senaru for official registration.
  • 08:00 AM: Hike through dense tropical rainforest rich in flora, birdlife, and butterflies.
  • POS 2 (1,500m): Take a refreshing break with hot tea, coffee, and biscuits.
  • POS 3 (2,000m): Enjoy a freshly prepared, warm hot lunch cooked by our team.
  • 04:00 PM: Arrive at Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m). Enjoy an incredible golden sunset over Mount Agung in Bali & Barujari volcano inside Segara Anak lake.
Standard Sharing Service: Group pickup, standard registration, team pace, shared tent & camp equipment.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private AC vehicle, fast-track registration, custom pacing, outdoor table/chairs for lunch, 8cm mattress, pillow & private toilet tent.
02
Day Two

Second Summit Push (3,126m) & Descent to Senaru

⏱️ Est. 7 - 8 Hours Hiking
  • 04:00 AM: Early wake up with hot drinks & toast, then begin the summit push to Sangkarang Peak (3,126m).
  • 06:00 AM: Reach Senaru Second Summit for an incredible sunrise over Rinjani peak & surrounding islands.
  • 07:30 AM: Descend back to Crater Rim camp for a full warm breakfast.
  • 09:30 AM: Pack up camp (Zero-Waste commitment) & descend back through Senaru rainforest. Keep an eye out for rare black lutung monkeys!
  • 02:30 PM: Arrive back at Senaru basecamp where your driver awaits for drop-off across Lombok.
Standard Sharing Service: Group guide summit pacing, cooked breakfast, shared ground transfer after trek.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Dedicated private guide for summit push, gourmet hot meals, private VIP AC transport drop-off & FREE Speedboat ticket to Gili Islands.

Why Choose the Senaru Second Summit Route?

Best Sunset Views

Facing west, the Senaru route offers the most iconic golden sunset views over the Gili Islands and Mount Agung in Bali.

Lush Rainforest Canopy

Most of the trail is shaded by a dense tropical canopy, making the trek cooler and protected from direct sunlight.

Less Crowded Alternative

A fantastic alternative to the main peak, offering a quiet, peaceful, yet highly challenging 3,126m high-altitude experience.
