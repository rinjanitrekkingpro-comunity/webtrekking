Bukit Pergasingan

The Ultimate Sunrise Viewpoint Over Sembalun Valley

Overview

Not quite ready to tackle the full Mount Rinjani trek? Bukit Pergasingan (Pergasingan Hill) is the perfect alternative. Standing at 1,700 meters above sea level, it offers a gentle 2–3 hour hike that rewards you with one of Lombok’s most spectacular sunrises: a sweeping patchwork of Sembalun’s farmlands stretching far below, crowned by the majestic silhouette of Mount Rinjani rising in the distance.

Pergasingan is ideal for travelers who want the thrill of mountain trekking without a multi-day grueling expedition—while still capturing world-class sunrise photographs.

What Makes It So Special

What sets Pergasingan apart is its unparalleled panoramic view over Sembalun Valley. From the summit, neatly arranged fields and farm plots form a stunning multicolored natural tapestry—lovingly called the "Patchwork Valley" by visitors. Layered before the commanding peak of Mount Rinjani, this scene makes Pergasingan one of Lombok’s most sought-after photography destinations.

In addition to its beauty, the trail is beginner-friendly—perfect as an acclimatization warm-up before attempting the full Rinjani summit, or simply to gauge your fitness readiness for higher-altitude treks.

Useful Tips

  • Bring warm layers: Temperatures at 1,700m before dawn can be surprisingly cold.
  • Carry a headlamp or flashlight: The majority of the ascent takes place in darkness.
  • Wear trekking shoes with good grip: Earthy trails may be slippery, especially after rain.
  • Bring drinking water and light snacks to keep energy levels steady during the 2–3 hour climb.
  • Best time to visit: During the dry season (April–October) for the clearest skies and best sunrise visibility.
