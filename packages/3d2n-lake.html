Package Overview

Designed for true adventurers, this 3 Days 2 Nights trekking package offers the classic full traverse experience of Mount Rinjani. You will start your journey from Sembalun Village, trek through open savanna grasslands, and push for the 3,726m summit at sunrise.

After reaching the top, descend into the volcano crater to camp by Segara Anak Lake. Soothe your muscles in natural hot springs, enjoy freshly cooked meals prepared by our porters, ascend to Senaru Rim, and finish your journey through the dense, shady tropical rainforest of Senaru—all guided under our 100% Zero-Waste policy.

Day by Day Journey

Detailed Trekking Itinerary

The classic 3-day Sembalun to Senaru traverse covering summit, lake, and hot springs.

01
Day One

Sembalun Basecamp to Sembalun Crater Rim

⏱️ Est. 7 - 8 Hours Hiking
  • Pickup from hotel & transfer to Sembalun Village (1,100m) for registration & medical check.
  • Trek through open savanna grasslands with clear views of Rinjani peak.
  • Stop at POS 2 or POS 3 for a freshly prepared hot trail lunch.
  • Ascend the steep "Regret Hills" up to Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m).
  • Set up camp, enjoy sunset dinner over clouds, and rest early for midnight summit.
Standard Sharing Service: Group pickup, standard tent & sleeping gear, group cooked meals.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private AC vehicle, fast-track registration, trail dining table & chairs, 8cm mattress, pillow & private toilet tent.
02
Day Two

Summit Attack (3,726m), Segara Anak Lake & Senaru Rim

⏱️ Est. 10 - 11 Hours Hiking
  • 02:00 AM wake up call for summit push (3,726m) to witness sunrise across Bali & Sumbawa.
  • Return to Crater Rim camp for a heavy breakfast recovery meal.
  • Descend rocky trail down into Segara Anak Crater Lake (2,000m).
  • Relax and soak in natural thermal hot springs beside the lake.
  • Ascend to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m) for second night camp & sunset view over Mount Agung.
Standard Sharing Service: Group summit pacing, hot springs break, standard camping setup.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Dedicated private guide for summit push, gourmet hot meals, deluxe 8cm mattress at Senaru Rim camp.
03
Day Three

Senaru Rim Descent via Tropical Rainforest

⏱️ Est. 5 - 6 Hours Hiking
  • Enjoy morning breakfast at Senaru Rim with views of Gili Islands and Bali.
  • Descend through the shady, dense tropical rainforest of Senaru.
  • Spot rare black lutung monkeys along the forest canopy.
  • Arrive at Senaru Village basecamp (~1:00 PM) for final lunch.
  • Transfer via car to your next destination (Airport, Senggigi, Kuta, or Harbor).
Standard Sharing Service: Shared vehicle drop-off across Lombok island.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private VIP AC vehicle drop-off, FREE Speedboat ticket to Gili Trawangan/Air/Meno, FREE Senaru Waterfall tour.
