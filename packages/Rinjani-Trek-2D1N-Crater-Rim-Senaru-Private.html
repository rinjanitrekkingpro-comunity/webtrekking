Package Overview

Looking for the best views of Mount Rinjani without pushing all the way to the summit? The 2 Days 1 Night Senaru Crater Rim package is the ultimate scenic choice. Starting from Senaru Village, you will trek through lush tropical rainforests up to the crater rim (2,641m) to spend an unforgettable night camping under the stars.

Senaru Crater Rim offers one of the most spectacular panoramas on the island: witness a magnificent golden sunset over Mount Agung in Bali, gaze down at Segara Anak Lake, and watch smoke rise from Mount Baru Jari volcano.

Day by Day Journey

Detailed Trekking Itinerary

Scenic 2-day hike through tropical rainforests to Senaru Crater Rim.

01
Day One

Tropical Forest Ascent to Senaru Crater Rim

⏱️ Est. 6 - 7 Hours Hiking
  • Morning registration at Senaru Rinjani Trekking Center (601m).
  • Begin trek through lush tropical rainforest rich in flora and wild butterflies.
  • Rest break at POS 2 Montong Satas (1,500m) for hot tea and biscuits.
  • Enjoy a freshly prepared hot lunch at POS 3 (2,000m).
  • Final ascent to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m) for a magnificent golden sunset over Mount Agung in Bali.
Standard Sharing Service: Group pickup, standard tent & sleeping gear, group cooked lunch.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private AC vehicle, fast-track registration, dining table & chairs on trail, 8cm thick mattress, pillow & private toilet tent.
02
Day Two

Crater Rim Sunrise & Forest Descent to Senaru

⏱️ Est. 4 - 5 Hours Hiking
  • Wake up to a sunrise over crater lake & smoking Baru Jari volcano with warm coffee.
  • Pack up camp (Zero-Waste policy) and descend through shady rainforest canopy.
  • Keep an eye out for rare black lutung monkeys in the trees.
  • Arrive back at Senaru basecamp by afternoon for final lunch.
  • Transfer via vehicle to your next destination across Lombok.
Standard Sharing Service: Shared car transfer to Senggigi, Mataram, Kuta, or Harbor.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private VIP AC vehicle drop-off, FREE Speedboat ticket to Gili Trawangan/Air/Meno, FREE Senaru Waterfall tour.
