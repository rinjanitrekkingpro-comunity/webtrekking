Package Overview

Short on time but hungry for the ultimate challenge? The 2 Days 1 Night Summit package is tailored for fit hikers who want to reach the 3,726m peak of Mount Rinjani. You will ascend via the Sembalun route, hiking through rolling savanna grasslands and tackling the steep crater rim ascent before making your midnight summit push.

Enjoy a majestic sunset from your crater rim camp, witness an epic sunrise from Lombok's highest point, and experience professional mountain hospitality operating under a strict 100% Zero-Waste policy.

Day by Day Journey

Detailed Trekking Itinerary

An express 2-day summit push via Sembalun savanna and crater rim.

01
Day One

Sembalun Basecamp to Pelawangan Crater Rim

⏱️ Est. 7 - 8 Hours Hiking
  • Morning transfer to Sembalun Village (1,100m) for official National Park registration.
  • Begin hiking through rolling savanna grasslands under open skies.
  • Stop at POS 2 or POS 3 for freshly prepared hot trail lunch.
  • Ascend the steep "Regret Hills" (Bukit Penyesalan) up to Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m).
  • Camp over the clouds, enjoy dinner, and rest early for the midnight summit push.
Standard Sharing Service: Group pickup, standard tent & sleeping gear, group lunch at POS 2/3.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private AC vehicle transfer, fast-track registration, dining table/chairs on trail, 8cm thick mattress & private toilet tent.
02
Day Two

Rinjani Summit Push (3,726m) & Descent to Basecamp

⏱️ Est. 10 - 12 Hours Hiking
  • 02:00 AM wake up call with hot tea/coffee and biscuits. Begin the steep, sandy summit push.
  • Reach the 3,726m summit by sunrise for a 360° view over Lombok, Bali, and Sumbawa.
  • Descend back to Crater Rim camp for a heavy, energizing breakfast.
  • Pack up camp (Zero-Waste policy) and descend back down the savanna trail to Sembalun Village.
  • Arrive at basecamp by mid-afternoon where transport awaits for drop-off at your next destination.
Standard Sharing Service: Standard group guide pacing, cooked breakfast, group car transfer after trek.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Dedicated private guide for summit push, gourmet hot breakfast, private VIP vehicle drop-off & FREE Gili speedboat ticket.
