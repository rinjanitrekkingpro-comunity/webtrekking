Tetebatu Walking Tour

Through Rice Terraces and Black Monkey Forests

Overview

Tucked away on the southern slopes of Mount Rinjani, Tetebatu is a peaceful mountain village of understated beauty. Famous for its endless tiers of vivid green rice terraces—often compared to those of Bali—it offers crisp mountain air, authentic rural life, and rare endemic flora and fauna found nowhere else.

This leisurely walking tour winds along narrow paths between rice paddies, where you will pass and greet local farmers tending their fields, before entering a lush forest sanctuary home to endemic Black Macaques: striking jet-black primates that are the region’s natural mascot.

Tour Highlights

  • Guided Walk through Rice Terraces: A gentle stroll along rice paddy edges, surrounded by layered green vistas that glow beautifully in the early morning when soft mist still drapes the valley.
  • Visit to the Monkey Forest: Observe Black Macaques in their natural habitat; these shy yet curious primates are easily spotted in the forest canopy around Tetebatu.
  • Sarang Walet Waterfall: A refreshing stop along the way, named for the many swiftlets that nest in the cliffs surrounding the falls.
  • Coffee, Vanilla & Spice Education: Visit a local plantation to see the traditional processing of Lombok-grown Robusta coffee, vanilla, and native spices—including a tasting session of fresh-brewed coffee straight from the farmers.

Why Tetebatu Is Worth Visiting

Unlike Lombok’s more crowded beach and mountain destinations, Tetebatu offers slow, restorative travel. Here, life moves at a gentle pace, surrounded by the calming sounds of flowing irrigation water and rustling leaves—an unforgettable escape from busy tourist hubs.

The area also supports extraordinary biodiversity: Lombok’s endemic birds, vibrant butterflies, and well-preserved highland tropical forests await discovery.

Useful Tips

  • Wear sturdy, comfortable footwear or trekking sandals—rice paddy paths can be damp and slippery.
  • Bring a hat and sunscreen; sections of the trail are open and unshaded.
  • Don’t forget your camera—the rice terraces framed by Mount Rinjani form one of Lombok’s most photographed views.
  • Be respectful of local customs and farmland; follow your guide’s directions for courtesy and safety.
  • Best time to visit: Early morning (7:00–10:00 AM), when the light is soft and temperatures are pleasantly cool.
