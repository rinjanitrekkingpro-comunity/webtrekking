Package Overview

Why rush when you can savor every moment? The 4 Days 3 Nights package offers the most comprehensive and relaxing way to explore Mount Rinjani National Park. By distributing the trekking hours evenly, this itinerary significantly reduces daily fatigue and allows ample time for photography, relaxing by Segara Anak Lake, and soaking in thermal hot springs.

You will conquer the 3,726m summit, spend an entire restful afternoon camping right beside the lake, watch golden sunsets from both crater rims, and descend through the dense, mystical rainforests of Senaru.

Day by Day Journey

Detailed Trekking Itinerary

Comprehensive 4-day traverse from Sembalun to Senaru with a relaxed daily schedule.

01
Day One

Sembalun Basecamp to Sembalun Crater Rim

⏱️ Est. 7 - 8 Hours Hiking
  • Register at Rinjani Trek Center in Sembalun (1,100m).
  • Enjoy a gentle hike across wide open savanna grasslands.
  • Stop for cooked hot trail lunch served by our porter team.
  • Ascend up to Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m).
  • Camp over clouds, enjoy sunset dinner, and rest early for summit push.
Standard Sharing Service: Group pickup, standard tent & sleeping gear, group cooked meals.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private AC vehicle, fast-track registration, dining table/chairs on trail, 8cm mattress, pillow & private toilet tent.
02
Day Two

Summit Attack (3,726m) & Camping at Segara Anak Lake

⏱️ Est. 8 - 9 Hours Hiking
  • 02:00 AM summit attack for an epic sunrise over Lombok, Bali & Sumbawa.
  • Return to Crater Rim camp for a full breakfast.
  • Descend into Segara Anak Crater Lake (2,000m) and set up camp.
  • Spend a relaxed afternoon swimming, soaking in thermal hot springs, or fishing with porters.
Standard Sharing Service: Group summit pacing, lakeside camp & hot springs break.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Dedicated private summit guide, deluxe lakeside camp setup with thick mattress & gourmet meals.
03
Day Three

Lakeside Morning to Senaru Crater Rim

⏱️ Est. 3 - 4 Hours Hiking
  • Enjoy a slow, peaceful morning by the crater lake without any rushing.
  • After lunch, begin the rocky 3-hour ascent to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m).
  • Set up camp for your final night overlooking Baru Jari volcano, Segara Anak Lake, and Mount Agung in Bali.
Standard Sharing Service: Standard group ascent to Senaru Rim, cooked dinner.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Gourmet rim dinner, tea/coffee service with sunset chairs, deluxe 8cm mattress.
04
Day Four

Senaru Rim Descent via Tropical Rainforest

⏱️ Est. 5 - 6 Hours Hiking
  • Descend through the dense, shady tropical rainforest of Senaru.
  • Enjoy a cool walk protected from sun exposure & spot black lutung monkeys.
  • Arrive at Senaru Village, receive your climbing certificate, and transfer to your next destination.
Standard Sharing Service: Shared vehicle drop-off across Lombok.
✨ VIP Private Upgrade: Private VIP AC vehicle transfer, FREE Speedboat ticket to Gili Trawangan/Air/Meno, FREE Senaru Waterfall tour.
