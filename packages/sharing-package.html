What's Included in Sharing Rates:

  • Licensed Team: Professional local mountain guide and porters.
  • Pre-Trek Stay: 1-night homestay in Senaru Village before hike.
  • Camping Equipment: Quality dome tent, sleeping bag, mattress, cooking gear & toilet tent.
  • Meals & Drinks: 3 freshly cooked daily meals, fruits, snacks, coffee/tea & water.
  • National Park Ticket: Official entry fees (IDR 250,000/person/day) & insurance.
  • Transfers: Pick-up before & drop-off after trek across all Lombok areas (Airport, Bangsal, Senggigi, Kuta, Mataram).
  • Luggage Storage: Free secure storage at Senaru basecamp.

Price Excludes:

  • Headlamp / Flashlight
  • Small Backpack (Daypack)
  • Hiking Shoes & Jacket
  • Guide & Porter Tipping
  • Extra Personal Luggage Porter
  • Trekking Poles

Important Note: Personal gear (shoes, warm jacket, headlamp, poles) can be easily rented at our Senaru basecamp prior to your hike.
