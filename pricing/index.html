3 Ways to Trek Rinjani: From Cheapest to Most Comfortable

All three use the same trails, the same licensed local guides and the same camping gear. The difference is who you trek with and how much we organise for you before and after the trek. Please read this before booking so you know exactly what you are paying for.

Important to know before you book

  • All prices are per person, not per group.
  • Sharing Group and Private prices depend on how many people are in your booking. Example: if 2 friends book the 2D1N Summit together, each person pays the "2-3 pax" price. Other hikers who join the same sharing group do not change your price.
  • "Sharing" means you trek together with other hikers who booked separately. You share the guide, porters, toilet tent and transport with them, and the group walks at a pace that works for everyone.
  • Trek Only does not include any transport. You need to arrive at the start of the trek in Senaru or Sembalun yourself, at the time we agree. If you need a car, ask us and we will tell you the price.
  • None of the levels include personal hiking gear (jackets, poles, headlamps), travel insurance or tips for the guide and porters. Gear can be rented at our basecamp.
  • Not sure which level suits you? Message us on WhatsApp and tell us your dates, group size and fitness, and we will recommend honestly.

Safety Procedures

Your safety is our ultimate priority. Trekking Mount Rinjani carries natural risks, but with careful preparation and an experienced local team, we minimize those risks so you can enjoy the hike.

Certified Guides

All our guides hold official certifications from the National Park and are trained in handling Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS) and basic mountain first aid.

Emergency Comms

Our team is equipped with two-way radios (HT) to monitor weather changes and communicate directly with basecamp and national park rescue teams.

Extra Logistics

We always pack emergency backup food and drinking water beyond the planned itinerary to prevent shortages in case of unexpected bad weather.

Evacuation SOP

We have clear, tested evacuation protocols in coordination with the National Park authorities should a hiker get injured or experience extreme exhaustion.
