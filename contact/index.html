Trek With Purpose

Payment Info & Our Commitment

When you trek with us, you are not just buying a tour. You are empowering the local community and protecting nature.

Booking & Payment

Low 10% Deposit

To lock in your dates and secure your expert guides and porters, we only require a minimum deposit of 10% from the total price. The remaining balance can be easily settled in cash upon arrival at our basecamp.

Group Special Promo!

Trekking is better together! If you gather a group of 10 people, we will give you 1 extra slot for FREE! (Total 11 hikers, but you only pay for 10). Perfect for student groups or large families.

Our Social Impact

Sharing with the Unfortunate

Your adventure changes lives. A portion of the profit from every booking is donated directly to orphans and underprivileged families in our local Sasak villages to help with food and education.

Beyond Mountain Cleanliness

Our eco-mission goes beyond bringing trash down from Mount Rinjani. We actively join forces with local environmental communities to conduct regular clean-ups in the villages surrounding the mountain, ensuring a healthy environment for the locals.
